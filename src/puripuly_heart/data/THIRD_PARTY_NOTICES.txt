Embedded native dependencies retain their own licenses. The original upstream
wheel, parent license and CycloneDX inventory are preserved without modification.

Native Xet transfers are enabled where compatible with the inherited transfer
preference and application TLS/proxy policy. Explicit custom CAs, incompatible
proxy bypass policies and mirrors retain HTTP; a failed native attempt retains
the existing HTTP fallback. The worker sets HF_HUB_DISABLE_TELEMETRY=1 and
