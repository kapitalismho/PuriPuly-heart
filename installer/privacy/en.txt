This policy covers the PuriPuly app and project-operated servers. External services have their own policies.

1. Usage statistics and your choice

- To estimate user numbers, the app sends a random identifier and the UTC activity date. The server stores a derived identifier with those dates, separately from account authentication. Statistics contain no conversations, audio, translations, or API keys.
- Statistics are on by default for new installations. Declining on the installer's Privacy Policy page disables them before first launch without blocking installation. Settings lets you change this later; upgrades preserve your choice.
- Turning statistics off stops transmission and removes the local identifier. Turning them back on creates a new one. Server records remain until cleanup, which removes activity more than 35 UTC days old; records for 36 dates including today may remain.

2. Audio, translation, and integrations

- Local models process data on your device. External recognition sends audio; external translation sends text and necessary context. Selected services and custom servers receive their configured authentication information. Their retention and training policies apply.
- New installations select Bilibili's no-key Index-Translate 35B API at index-translate.bilibili.com. Startup and selection send a synthetic availability request with no conversation content, even when translation is off. Ordinary translations may send the same request twice. Unavailability switches the saved selection to Gemma 4 Managed, or Gemma 4 OpenRouter if the Managed allowance is confirmed exhausted, using that route's normal authentication and processing. Free availability is not guaranteed.
- Managed translation goes to OpenRouter and its inference provider with a user reference, not through the project's authentication server. Choose local models or disable the feature to avoid external processing.
- VRChat context can include participant counts, not names or raw logs. OSC sends subtitles and controls to the configured destination and discovers and advertises services on the local network during automatic connection. Its default destination is local; Settings lets you change or disable it.

3. Managed accounts and security

Discord, QQ, and managed services process authentication data, account references, installation/device identifiers, and hardware hashes for access, entitlements, key delivery, and abuse prevention. Server records include entitlements, key issuance, rewards, and IP-based security information. These are separate from optional statistics and have different retention periods. Activity and key-issuance aggregates go to an operational Discord channel.

4. Network activity even with statistics off

The app checks GitHub for updates at startup and downloads models from services such as Hugging Face or ModelScope. Authentication and external features communicate independently of statistics. Local models and disabled statistics do not make the app fully offline.

Update/download requests contain no statistics identifier, conversations, or audio, but recipients and Cloudflare infrastructure process IP addresses and ordinary request metadata. Deleting server records does not immediately remove backups or records from earlier versions.

5. Local information and deletion

Settings, models, and logs are stored mainly in %LOCALAPPDATA%\puripuly-heart. Runtime logs include recognized text and translations, with limits on record length. Basic and Detailed logging both mask known secret patterns, but sensitive prose may remain. Review and redact logs before sharing.

Credentials are stored in Windows Credential Manager or encrypted files. Removing the app data folder does not remove all credentials or external-service records.

6. Puri assistant and voluntary support reports

Assistant requests

Puri starts when you send a message, without a separate consent dialog. Messages, necessary conversation/tool results, and allowlisted app-state and diagnostic summaries pass through the project's relay to OpenAI. Voice transcripts, translations, audio, raw logs, complete settings, and keys are not automatically included.

Enter keys only in secure Settings fields, never chat. Known-pattern blocking cannot detect every secret. Puri history stays in app memory, separate from voice transcripts. Reset can retain this app run's report archive; restarting clears it.

Broker authenticates the installation and issues a short-lived token. Relay and OpenAI receive a derived installation identifier (HMAC), not the raw ID. Required usage/cost/error accounting and random prompt-turn identifiers are separate from optional statistics. Daily limits count submitted prompt turns, not model calls or cost. Ordinary relay storage and logs retain no conversation content, setting values, or raw diagnostics.

OpenAI store:false does not mean Zero Data Retention. According to its data controls, checked 2026-10-30:
- API data is not used for training unless opted in.
- Abuse-monitoring content can remain up to 30 days, or longer for legal or harm-prevention reasons.
- Encrypted prompt-cache tensors can remain up to 24 hours.
- Special retention controls require provider approval.
https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data

Voluntary support reports

Only clicking Send beside the disclosure uploads a report. A suggestion alone creates or uploads nothing. Reports contain:
- This app run's entire Puri conversation, including before resets, tool calls/results, and notices; reasoning data is excluded.
- Only the current app log, including recognized text and translations.
- Metadata.

Masking cannot remove all sensitive prose. Reports go to developer-only R2 storage with a 30-day lifecycle; deletion normally follows within about 24 hours after expiry. Discord notifications contain only the ticket, app version, compressed size, and diagnostic codes.

Reports are limited to 25 MB compressed and three per installation per UTC day, separately from model usage. Failed delivery requires an explicit retry.
