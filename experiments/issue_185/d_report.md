Re-query inventory before trusting indices: the observed #21 Virtual Desktop WASAPI input still has max 1 channel / 48 kHz default; #33 Realtek USB microphone WDM-KS still has max 2 / 44.1 kHz default. The probe checks the expected name and host API; its JSON records all input inventory, resolved request, metadata channel decision and open attempts, effective channels, stream estimates, callback counts/sizes/clocks, source progression and distributions. Four sequential alternating omitted/low/omitted/low trials per device, 3 s WASAPI or 2 s Realtek each. Production Self's `determine_self_mic_capture_channels` selects min(device maximum, 2) with internal mono; first open requests that width, and **only if that open fails** retries mono (no rerouting for this same-device comparison). Here all first opens succeeded: WASAPI requested/opened/frame **1/1/1**, Realtek **2/2/2**, no mono retries. Actual `SoundDeviceAudioSource` requests `samplerate=None`, float32, `blocksize=0`, queue capacity 64, no WASAPI extra flags; wrapper supplies only `latency="low"` on alternate opens. Actual normalizer takes each source frame's channels, mixes stereo to mono before resampling at SoXR MQ, then assembles 512 samples at 16 kHz.

**Corrected frontier:** production `CaptureMappedStreamingResampler.process()` supplies mapped normalized spans. The probe uses the actual VAD owner's `_capture_prefix` on its accumulated span list for **each** completed 512-sample block, with the consumed prefix's last `source_end_monotonic_s`, and retains the remainder. A 3,763-frame/85-ms callback can complete multiple blocks with distinct frontiers; on discontinuity it discards partial accumulation as the VAD owner does. The observed timestamp is immediately after normalizer processing and prefix availability, not actual Silero invocation, acoustic sample age, an await of a fake callback, or physical presentation. Callback-host anchoring and intra-chunk span interpolation are **not calibrated acoustic timestamps**; pre-callback age remains unknown. A deterministic 480+3,360 normalized-sample replay produced seven distinct mapped 512 frontiers and a 256-sample remainder.

All eight runs reported zero source statuses, zero callback-queue drops, zero unknown discontinuities, and admitted-source-end equal to next-source-sample (no known lost range). Realtek high has 29 admitted frames out of 30 observed callbacks per run; Realtek low 241/243; therefore raw callback-index pairing cannot safely reconstruct an age for every callback. The zero statuses and drops describe these brief idle windows only; interval p95 variation, including WASAPI low first trial's 20.01 ms, is visible rather than suppressed. Opened `stream.latency` is a driver estimate, not calibrated acoustic age. The Realtek *post-callback mapped-frontier* median changed by ~39.5 ms across the high/low profiles, while the WASAPI modes are similar; neither comparison proves end-to-end or pre-callback improvement.

**Clock validity:** zero usable `inputBufferAdcTime`/`currentTime` pairs in all corrected trials, so **no valid PA end-frontier input-age field conclusion is available**. WASAPI ADC exceeds currentTime on all 1,196 callbacks (299 per run), despite host minus PA currentTime median ~.019–.020 ms and small within-run offset drift. Realtek ADC starts at zero (one nonpositive per run) then advances in a different apparent domain from process-monotonic currentTime (29/242 invalid per high/low run); the apparent many-thousand-second delta is rejected, not a microphone age. Realtek host-minus-current medians .020–.054 ms do not validate its ADC. Reject reversed ordering and >1 s apparent-domain mismatch; never substitute either host/PA clock as an acoustic calibration or change canonical source timestamps.

**Historical evidence explicitly separated:** [earlier mono WASAPI](d_historical_mono_wasapi.json) and [earlier mono Realtek](d_historical_mono_realtek.json) remain metadata-only observations on original revision `78d90ca9722d3c88e05448bbe7c958892c5b11ec`. Their `source_to_vad_ready_proxy_ms` used **one full mapped chunk end** when accumulated output crossed 512, then `available %= 512`; it assigned later source ends to earlier blocks and missed extra blocks in the same large callback. The old ~10 ms WASAPI / ~26 ms Realtek-high figures are **withdrawn as VAD-frontier estimates**, not comparable to this corrected field. Earlier ADC-invalid observations and latency/cadence remain historical for their exact mono settings; the Realtek mono runs are **not** evidence of production's successful stereo request.

**Disposition:** keep existing omitted-latency open request and canonical capture clock. The physical Realtek device shows a scoped low-request candidate for investigation, not approval of a universal or device-specific production default, fallback, fixed block size, timestamp change, or VAD setting. Maintainer decision requires representative VRChat-load stability/compatibility evidence and a validated device-specific pre-callback sample-age reference (for example verified loopback calibration or host-driver clock interpretation). HMD capture/display and other devices remain untested; F's separate queue/VAD evidence is not rerun or reinterpreted here.
