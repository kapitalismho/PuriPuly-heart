The pressured producer continued pacing while the consumer fell behind: 16 resumed-frame `known_loss` ranges in [`f_results.json`](f_results.json), totaling **48 × 480 = 23,040 source samples**, equal to `queue_drop_count=48`; no unknown gaps or trailing unobserved drops in this run. For example callback 139 resumes at source sample 66,720 with 960 missing samples; callback 147 resumes at 70,560 with 1,920 missing. `PhysicalCaptureProgression` advances physical source coordinates *before* `put_nowait`; it does not compress lost samples. `run_audio_vad_loop` observes the known discontinuities and resets affected normalization/VAD continuity. No synthetic missing-time silence. The normal run had no drops and no gaps. Production loopback/process queues share 64-frame defaults and progression semantics but this probe does not run those physical producers: 64 × 1,024/48,000 = 1.365 s is loopback *stored audio capacity*, not idle delay. Our synthetic queue stores at most 64 × 480/48,000 = 640 ms of **accepted** audio, yet the oldest retained packet exceeded 1.27 s of age while **new** packets were rejected. Capacity in audio duration does not cap wall-clock age under drop-new pressure.

The mic idle field observation from D's actual WASAPI input saw 0 callback status/queue drops, 480-frame mode, ~0.14–0.23 ms queue-age p95 over four 3-second runs. It is a separate **idle** hardware observation, not a loaded F trace. No VRChat or representative loaded process was available; therefore field relevance and live loopback/process tails remain **unknown**. F's `source→VAD` age includes normalizer phase and queued frames, not hidden pre-callback acoustic age (D's PA ADC clocks were unusable). Scheduling lag is measured against the paced thread's planned deadline and not added blindly to queue age.

**Disposition:** existing nonblocking callback, finite 64-slot queue and known-loss accounting are sufficient for the source/discontinuity contract in these runs (no demonstrated violation), but the pressure run shows an avoidable-staleness *candidate* only. Retaining newest instead of oldest would require a separately approved policy specifying which not-yet-submitted range is retired, exactly-once failure/discontinuity handling, immutable submitted range protection and effect on VAD/segment ownership; no threshold, capacity reduction, or silent drop implemented. A representative VRChat-load or physical loopback/process trace is the exact remaining prerequisite for choosing a field freshness policy. Maintainer owns any new policy decision.
